About YouTube Metadata Studio

This custom application extracts complete metadata, compliance status, and high-definition assets directly from YouTube videos without requiring API keys.

  • HD Thumbnails: Download 1080p, 720p, 480p, or 360p images with 1 click.
  • Made for Kids Check: Inspect COPPA compliance status.
  • Playlist / List Name: Detects and displays the playlist title or series name.
  • Individual Copy: Every field has an individual copy button.
  • Interactive Tags: Click any tag pill to copy just that keyword.
  • Export: Download all data as JSON, Plain Text, or Markdown.
